All Blu Idol demi-fine jewellery items are engineered using advanced Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) 18K gold plating over solid stainless steel cores:

  • Waterproof & Sweatproof: Our jewellery is built to withstand sweat, high tropical Kerala humidity, fresh water, and daily showers without discoloring or fading.

  • Jewellery Care Advice: While our jewellery is highly durable, we advise avoiding direct contact with harsh industrial chemicals, strong bleaches, or abrasive cleaners. Gently wipe with a soft dry cloth after prolonged wear to retain brilliant luster.
