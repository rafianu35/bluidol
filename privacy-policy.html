Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. Learn how Blu Idol collects, utilizes, and safeguards your personal information with absolute confidentiality and care.

Home / Privacy Policy
Effective Date: September 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Blu Idol ("we", "us", "our", or the "Brand"), operating through our website https://www.bluidol.in. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy in strict compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) of India.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our storefront, communicate with our WhatsApp concierge, or place an order, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Contact Information: Name, WhatsApp phone number, calling number, and email address.
  • Delivery & Billing Details: Street address, city, district, state (e.g. Kerala), PIN code, and landmark required for courier dispatch.
  • Order & Transaction Details: Specific jewellery items selected, order reference codes, total amounts paid, and payment gateway confirmation identifiers.
  • Customer Communications: Messages, feedback, sizing questions, and product inquiries exchanged with our concierge team.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: IP address, device type, operating system, browser details, and anonymous page interaction telemetry collected via Google Analytics 4.

Strict Payment Data Protection

We do NOT store or have access to your sensitive financial credentials. All digital payments are processed through certified, RBI-licensed payment gateways. Your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, and banking passwords are never visible to or retained on our servers.

2. How We Collect Your Data

We collect information directly from you through the following touchpoints:

  • When you complete an order inquiry or purchase on bluidol.in.
  • When you initiate a chat or send product links to our official WhatsApp support number (+91 9895432549).
  • When you subscribe to our newsletter or enter your email for promotional vouchers.
  • Automatically through standard HTTP cookies, local storage (used to remember your cart items and wishlist preferences), and Google Tag Manager.

3. Purpose & Use of Collected Information

Your information is used strictly to provide you with an exceptional shopping experience, specifically to:

  • Process, package, and dispatch your jewellery orders with our certified logistics partners.
  • Provide real-time WhatsApp order confirmations, dispatch tracking numbers, and delivery notifications.
  • Answer customer support requests, sizing consultations, and warranty inquiries.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and protect the integrity of our platform.
  • Optimize site loading speed, user interface design, and product catalog relevance across Indian mobile devices.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information under any circumstances.

Your information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with trusted service partners exclusively to fulfill your orders:

  • Logistics Partners: Certified express couriers (e.g. Delhivery, India Post, Blue Dart, DTDC) receive your name, delivery address, and phone number solely to deliver your parcel.
  • Payment Processors: Secure financial institutions facilitating payment settlement.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly mandated by Indian law, statutory court orders, or governmental law enforcement agencies.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses lightweight browser cookies and HTML5 local storage. These technologies allow your shopping cart to persist across tabs, retain items on your wishlist, and generate anonymous site traffic statistics via Google Analytics. You may disable cookies at any time through your browser settings; however, certain cart and wishlist conveniences may be limited.

6. Data Security Measures

We implement rigorous technical and administrative security standards, including full-site 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption, secure edge CDN hosting, and restricted access protocols to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

7. Your Privacy Rights

As a valued customer, you have full control over your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold regarding your account.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct any incomplete or outdated delivery details.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of your customer history, subject to statutory tax and invoice retention obligations.
  • Opt-Out of Marketing: You can opt out of newsletter emails or WhatsApp promotional broadcasts at any time by replying "STOP" or emailing us.

8. Grievance Officer & Contact Information
