Frequently Asked questions

Everything you need to know about our 18K gold plated pieces, materials, skin safety, and WhatsApp ordering.

In Blu Idol, BLU stands for Bring Luxury to You (B = Bring, L = Luxury, U = to You). Our mission is to make exquisite, lab-verified demi-fine jewellery accessible for everyday wear without extreme luxury markups.

We use state-of-the-art 18K Gold Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) vacuum plating over premium surgical-grade 316L stainless steel and jewelry brass. Our stones are precision-cut AAA+ cubic zirconia and simulated royal sapphire crystals that retain their deep sparkle.

Yes, 100%. All Blu Idol pieces are certified hypoallergenic, nickel-free, and lead-free. They are specifically crafted for people with delicate or allergy-prone skin, so you'll never experience rashes, itching, or green discoloration.
